About

Research with business impact

I am a scientist and researcher working across biotech and biopharma, with a strong foundation in molecular biology, biochemistry, and therapeutic development. My work spans NGS sequencing, gene editing, gene therapy, and viral vector development, with a focus on translating biological insight into drug discovery and translational innovation.

I work at the intersection of wet-lab science and data analysis, using Python and R to support reproducible workflows, interpret complex biological data, and accelerate translational research. I am especially motivated by programs that connect scientific discovery to real-world therapeutic impact.
